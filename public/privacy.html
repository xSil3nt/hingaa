hingaa prototype privacy

This local prototype sends place-search text, selected locations and walking endpoints to Google Maps Platform to provide search, maps and walking directions. If you choose current location, that coordinate is used for your requested journey.

Bus route codes are sent to RTL for arrivals and vehicle positions. The prototype has no accounts, advertising or analytics. hingaa endpoints and walking results are held temporarily in server memory for the active planning session, for up to five minutes; restarting the server clears them. Google and RTL may process requests under their own policies.

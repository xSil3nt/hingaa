hingaa prototype

hingaa is an independent local team prototype for Greater Malé bus planning. Boarding predictions and published times come from RTL when available. Bus ride durations are modelled. Walking directions are supplied by Google Maps when available, with an explicitly labelled OpenStreetMap fallback.

Check real-world crossings, pedestrian access and bus service before travelling. Connections and arrival times are estimates. Tickets are not sold here.

Google Maps features are subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

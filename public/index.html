About this prototype

Journey uses RTL’s published stops, route order and road shapes, plus live stop arrivals when available. It is an independent team prototype.

Timing: boarding uses a catchable live estimate or an upcoming published time. The walk and bus ride durations are modelled. A total is only an estimate; transfers aren’t guaranteed.

Allow 2 minutes at every boarding, in addition to walking. Missing departures show a journey outline with no total arrival promise.

Place search and walking directions use Google Maps when available; otherwise walking follows public paths in OpenStreetMap. Short connections to the map are approximate; entrances, crossings and accessibility have not been surveyed. Check conditions on the ground.

Vehicle dots are fetched positions. RTL does not supply a GPS-fix timestamp, heading or direction. A nearby dot alone does not identify a usable bus.

Flat fares are summed per bus boarding. Distance-based fares show “Confirm fare”. No ticket purchases are included.
